How to Activate Levels & Start Earning

Level 1 (Iron Box) is completely FREE upon registration! Open Level 1, tap your 6 daily taps to collect 5 Ksh per tap (total 30 Ksh/day). After finishing your 6 daily taps, a 12-hour countdown begins before you can tap again. For higher levels, pay via Lipa na M-Pesa (Activation Fee + User Deposit Fee). Once approved by Customer Support, your deposit fee is instantly credited to your dashboard balance available for withdrawal, and your level unlocks!
